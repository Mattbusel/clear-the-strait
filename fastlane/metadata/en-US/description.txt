An enormous, extremely round, extremely pleased-with-himself cartoon politician has wedged himself sideways in a shipping channel. The cargo ships are queuing. Somebody has to move him.

That somebody is you, and your only tool is shoving.

FREE TO PLAY. NO ADVERTS.

HOW IT WORKS
Tap the left of him to shove him right, the right to shove him left. Shove in time with the way he is already rocking and the combo climbs: GOOD, GREAT, PERFECT, TREMENDOUS. Mash at random and you fight your own momentum.

THE PROBLEM IS HIS EGO
Every few seconds he announces how tremendous he is, and the compliment floats off as a speech bubble. Then it turns around and comes back. If it reaches him, he believes it: rounder, heavier, more stuck. Pop it first and he shrinks. Shove him, or pop his ego. You cannot do both.

THE PAYOFF
Build enough momentum and he pops out of the channel and skips across open water like a beach ball, still talking, while the distance counter climbs. Let him inflate on purpose and he flies further, if you can still budge him.

NEW: THE DAILY STRAIT
A fresh challenge every day, the same for everyone: a condition (Fog Bank, Big Lunch, Hot Air, Echo Canyon...) and a goal to beat. Beat it day after day to build a streak. One free try a day, and free play is always unlimited.

WHAT IS NOT IN HERE
No accounts, no sign-up, no adverts, no subscription, no internet connection required. Nothing is collected about you.

A NOTE ON THE SATIRE
These are original cartoon caricatures of generic types (the self-important public figure, the windbag, the tycoon, the small-town mayor) in made-up places. No real person is portrayed or named, and nothing anyone has actually said is quoted. Nobody is harmed: you are rolling a cartoon ball out of the way.
