PRIVACY POLICY

Clear the Strait. Last updated 10 September 2026.

The short version

The game collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores nothing about you. It has no network code of its own.

What is stored

Two numbers on your device: your best launch distance and your fastest clear time. They live in the app's own storage, never leave the phone, and are deleted with the app.

What is collected

Nothing. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, no account, no login, and no server belonging to this app. Because nothing is collected, nothing is shared, sold, or linked to you.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you choose to sign in, leaderboard scores are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. Declining changes nothing about the game.

Children

No data is collected from anyone, including children.
