CLEAR THE STRAIT

He is stuck. Shove him out.

Support

Something broken, or an idea? Email mattbusel@gmail.com and mention which iPhone you are on.

How to play

Tap the left of him to shove him right, the right to shove him left. Shove in time with the way he is already rocking to build a combo. Pop the speech bubbles before they get back to him, or he inflates and wedges himself in harder. Build enough momentum and he launches across the water.

Questions

Why can't I move him? His ego is too high. Pop bubbles for a while and the escape bar under the ego meter will start filling much faster.

Does mashing work? No. Roughly half of a mash shoves against his motion and kills your momentum. Watch him and tap in rhythm.

Do I need an internet connection? No. Game Center leaderboards are optional; everything else works offline.
