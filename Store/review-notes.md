# App Review notes

Pasted into the "Notes" field of the App Review Information section. Also
supplied programmatically by `Store/listing.py review-details`.

---

No account, login or network connection is required. Tap PLAY and the game starts immediately.

HOW TO PLAY: choose a character in the row above PLAY (the first is free), then tap PLAY. Tap the left side of the character to shove him right, the right side to shove him left. Shoving in time with his rocking builds a combo. Speech bubbles drift away and return; tapping one pops it. With enough momentum he is launched and the result screen appears. A round lasts about 45 to 120 seconds.

NEW IN 1.2
- Daily Strait: the card above the character row. A fixed daily condition and goal (computed from the date on the device), one free try a day, and a daily streak. Free play stays unlimited.
- Medals, launch trails, records and a calendar in "the Locker": the yellow medal button at the top right of the title screen.
- Postcards: on the result screen, POSTCARD draws a shareable picture of the launch (the first is free).

IN-APP PURCHASES (StoreKit 2; all optional; Locker > Shop lists every item with its price, plus Restore Purchases)
- 3 Daily Retries (consumable, $0.99): after the free daily try, tapping the Daily Strait card opens a sheet offering them. Each is one more attempt at that day's challenge; unused ones carry over.
- 2 Streak Shields (consumable, $0.99): spent automatically to cover a missed day so the daily streak survives. One free each month.
- 3 Postcards (consumable, $0.99): offered from the result screen once the free postcard is used.
- Trail Pack (non-consumable, $0.99): three cosmetic launch trails (Locker > Trails).
- Icon Pack (non-consumable, $0.99): three alternate app icons, chosen in Locker > Shop after purchase.
- The Blowhard Pack (existing non-consumable, unchanged).
No randomized rewards, no loot boxes, nothing gameplay-affecting is sold; every item says exactly what it gives.

ABOUT THE CONTENT: an original satirical cartoon of a generic, unnamed, extremely round self-important public figure stuck in a fictional, unnamed waterway. No real person, country, place, conflict, organisation or event appears or is referenced; no flags or military vessels; every line is written for this game and none is presented as a real quotation. There is no violence or peril: the whole interaction is pushing a cartoon ball out of the way, and he is cheerful throughout.

AGE RATING: 4+. No violence, profanity, gambling, user-generated content, chat, web access or advertising.

PRIVACY: no data of any kind is collected. No analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifier or server. Personal bests, the career record (medals, daily streak, credits) and settings are stored on the device in UserDefaults (privacy manifest CA92.1).
